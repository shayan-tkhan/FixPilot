# FixPilot workflow narration

From scattered messages to one tracked issue

Campus faults get lost in scattered messages. Students cannot see who owns the repair, while staff chase updates across departments.

1. Report the fault

FixPilot starts with one report. A student describes the fault, selects a location, and can attach a photo or video.

2. Review and route

Staff review the details and possible duplicates, then route the issue. AI suggestions are optional, and unavailable analysis never blocks a manual report.

3. Give the work an owner

The issue gets a named technician and a repair estimate. Response countdowns flag unanswered assignments, so work has a clear owner.

4. Record the repair

The technician starts work and records progress. Before and after evidence, or a completion note, gives staff something concrete to review.

5. Independently review

Completing a task does not close it automatically. Department staff inspect the repair and approve it, or return it for more work.

6. Let the student decide

The student makes the final check. They confirm the problem is solved, or reopen the same report if it is still broken.

Every handoff stays visible

One timeline connects every handoff. Campus maps and monthly summaries give staff an overview, without replacing staff review or student confirmation.
